# Cyberpunk

High technology, low trust. Corporations own the skyline, the street finds its own uses for things, and everyone is selling something.

- People: fixers, couriers, medics with no licence, burned-out officers, middle managers in over their heads, gang lieutenants, true believers. Each has an angle and someone leaning on them.
- Places: night markets, arcologies, flooded districts, server farms, bars with a back room. Say who provides security and who is watching.
- Trouble: a job with a hidden client, a double-cross, stolen data that changes hands, a neighbourhood about to be cleared.
- Rewards: money that is spent quickly, gear, a clean identity, leverage, a debt cancelled.
- Style: clipped and sensory: neon, rain, brand names you invent. Keep invented slang light and consistent.
