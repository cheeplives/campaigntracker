# Modern supernatural

Today's world with something hidden under it. The secret has rules, factions and a cost, and ordinary life goes on around it.

- People: police who file it under unexplained, shop owners who know what to sell, old families, new converts, things that pass for human. Give each a public face and a private one.
- Places: real-feeling streets, night buses, basements, parks after dark, institutions with a second purpose.
- Trouble: a breach of the secret's rules, a turf dispute, a bargain coming due, someone innocent caught in it.
- Rewards: favours, sanctuary, knowledge, a name to call on.
- Style: grounded and contemporary. Keep the supernatural consistent with the campaign's established rules, and never use real living people.
