# Post-apocalyptic

The old world ended and people are making do in what is left. Scarcity drives everything, and communities matter more than loners.

- People: scavengers, caravan bosses, settlement councils, medics, raiders with a code, people who remember before. Say what each needs to get through the season.
- Places: fortified settlements, ruins with something still worth taking, dead roads, trading posts. Note water, food, defences and what the place is short of.
- Trouble: shortages, sickness, a broken machine nobody can fix, a rival group with the same need, a rumour of somewhere better.
- Rewards: food, fuel, ammunition, medicine, parts, a place inside the walls.
- Style: spare and practical. Count things. Hope should be possible but cost effort.
