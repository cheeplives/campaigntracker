# Grim low fantasy

Mud, debt and hard choices. Magic is rare, feared and costs something. Nobody is simply a hero, and violence has consequences that last.

- People: tired officials, mercenaries between contracts, smugglers, zealots, nobles with thin purses, ordinary folk protecting their own. Everyone owes someone.
- Places: crowded walled towns, toll roads, failing villages, battlefields picked clean. Note who holds power there and who pays for it.
- Trouble: feuds, plague, hunger, corruption, a small evil nobody will act on. Dilemmas beat monsters; when there is a monster it is singular and dreadful.
- Rewards: silver, a favour, a safe winter, a secret worth holding. Rarely anything clean.
- Style: terse and dry, facts first. Understatement over gore. Injuries, weather and money matter.
