# Space opera

Starships, strange worlds and big gestures. Travel is fast enough for adventure, and politics, trade and old wars shape every port.

- People: captains, fixers, envoys, pilgrims, engineers, pirates, officers of rival powers, aliens with their own customs. Give each a loyalty and a price.
- Places: stations, frontier colonies, derelicts, court worlds, markets where everything is for sale. Say what a place exports, who runs it and what is wrong there.
- Trouble: a job that is more than it seemed, a faction squeeze, a discovery others will kill for, a debt on the ship.
- Rewards: credits, cargo, upgrades, standing with a faction, a star chart nobody else has.
- Style: vivid and fast. Technology works unless the story needs it to fail; describe what it does, not how.
