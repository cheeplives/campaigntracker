# Heroic fantasy

Capable heroes, visible magic, old ruins and clear stakes. The tone is adventurous and warm, with room for wonder and for humour.

- People: innkeepers, guild masters, knights, hedge wizards, priests of rival temples, travelling merchants, monsters with reasons of their own. Give each a want and a manner of speaking.
- Places: market towns, borderland keeps, wild forests, dungeons with a history. Say what a place was built for and what lives there now.
- Trouble: a threat that grows if ignored, a villain with a plan, a treasure with a claim on it. Offer more than one way through: a fight, a bargain, a trick.
- Rewards: coin, magic items with a quirk, titles, allies, land.
- Style: concrete and brisk. Prefer a vivid detail to a long history. Invented names should sound like they belong together.
