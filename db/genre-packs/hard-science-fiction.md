# Hard science fiction

A future that could be built. Distance, fuel, air and time are real constraints, and institutions matter as much as individuals.

- People: crew specialists, administrators, contractors, researchers, union stewards, auditors. Competent people under pressure, with employers and families.
- Places: habitats, outposts, ships on long transits, industrial sites. Note life support, who owns the place and how long help would take to arrive.
- Trouble: failures with causes, shortages, conflicting orders, a cover-up, a decision with no good answer and a clock on it.
- Rewards: pay, a renewed contract, data, political cover, survival with the hull intact.
- Style: precise and plain. Keep physics and travel times consistent with what the campaign has established; if a number isn't known, say so rather than inventing one.
