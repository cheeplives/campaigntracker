# Historical

A real period, played straight or with a light twist. The texture of the time (rank, faith, trade, law, distance) shapes what people can do.

- People: merchants, clergy, soldiers, servants, officials, artisans, travellers from elsewhere. Place each in the period's order: who they answer to and who answers to them.
- Places: towns, estates, ports, roads, frontiers. Note who holds authority, what is traded and how far news travels.
- Trouble: inheritance, patronage, war, religion, crime, a letter in the wrong hands.
- Rewards: money, office, a marriage, protection, reputation.
- Style: plausible for the period the campaign has set, without lecturing. Invented people are fine; don't put invented words or deeds on real historical figures unless the GM has. If a period detail is uncertain, say so.
